Privacy Policy

Homelab Assistant is a personal, single-user tool. It accesses only the Google Calendar data of its owner, using credentials the owner controls.

  • Calendar data is used solely to read and manage the owner's own events.
  • No data is shared with, sold to, or accessible by any third party.
  • All data processing happens on the owner's own private server.
  • Access can be revoked at any time via Google Account permissions.
